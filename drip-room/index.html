THE DRIP ROOM · v2.0 · 2026 · verified pass Built for nurses transitioning into ICU practice. Framework: Why this drip → what am I accomplishing → how do I know it's working → what can go wrong → what do I document. Dose figures carry a tier badge: Reference-verified (FDA label, society guideline, or Bojar), Unit practice (local convention — real, but not a published number), or Verification pending. Where sources conflict or data are absent, the app says so instead of picking. Drug data lives in a single JSON object (DRUGS) — extend it to add agents or unit settings.
