Post-Op Bleeding — Quick Algorithm

Educational quick reference — not a substitute for institutional policy, blood bank protocol, or surgeon/APP judgment. Thresholds are institution-dependent; defer to your printed protocol & cardiac anesthesia.
1 · Risk? Long CPB >150m · redo · root/arch/DHCA · antiplatelets/AC · plt<50k · ↓fibrinogen · renal/liver
2 · Bleeding? Normal trends DOWN: ~2→1→<0.5 mL/kg/hr by 12h. Investigate sustained >100 mL/hr.

Surgical bleed

Bright red, won't slow; clots then sudden stop; HTN/shiver/agitation; normal coags & TEG.
→ Call surgeon early · resuscitate · prep re-exploration

Coagulopathy

Diffuse ooze, drainage absent of clot; ↑PT/PTT, ↓fibrinogen/plt; hypothermia, CPB>150m.
→ Fix basics · targeted products (give map) · recheck

Mixed

High output AND ooze; partial response then re-bleeds.
→ Correct coagulopathy + keep surgical suspicion high

Retained / Tamponade ⚠

Sudden drop/STOP in output + ↓BP, ↑CVP, narrow pulse pressure, ↑pressors.
→ Call NOW · urgent echo · emergent OR / drainage
Fix the basics first
Warm → normothermia Correct iCa (Factor IV) Treat HTN / pain / shiver Confirm tube patency Protamine : heparin <1.0

Guideline notes (2021 STS · 2024 EACTS)
VET-guided algorithm is recommended. PCC preferred over FFP for coagulopathic bleeding. Fibrinogen if <150 mg/dL with bleeding (some target ≥200). Antifibrinolytics also prophylactic. No prophylactic FFP/DDAVP/rFVIIa. rFVIIa = rescue only.

Call surgeon / APP NOW

  • Won't slow despite warmth, iCa, products · bright red / clots in tubing
  • Sudden drop/stop in output + hemodynamic decline · ↑CVP + ↓BP + ↑pressors

Post-op ICU bleeding =

  • >200 mL/hr × 2 consecutive hr
  • OR Hgb drop ≥ 2 g/dL per hour

Return-to-OR territory (surgeon call)

  • >400 mL/hr · >300 mL/hr × 2–3 hr · >200 mL/hr × 4 hr
  • Hand off: output trend + character · vitals/pressors · CVP · Hgb/plt/fibrinogen/INR-PTT/iCa · temp · TEG
